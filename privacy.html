Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

The short version: seva stores what you need to keep your wellness list and share it with friends. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account and data at any time in the app.

This policy explains what information the seva iOS app ("seva", "we", "us") collects, how it's used, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. seva is operated by Aarushi Parashar.

Information you give us

  • Account details. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive a private relay address instead of your real email. For Sign in with Apple, we also keep a token from Apple so we can revoke seva's access when you delete your account.
  • Profile. Your display name and username, which you can change in Profile → Edit profile.
  • Your list. The practitioners and places you save, whether you've been or want to try them, your ratings, the provider you saw, the service, visit date and price level, how you found them (for example "friend rec" and who recommended them), any notes you add, and custom lists you create.
  • Friends. The people you follow, and anyone you block.
  • Contacts (only if you tap "Find friends"). We check your contacts' email addresses against seva accounts. Addresses are scrambled (hashed) on your phone before anything is sent, and we don't store your contacts or the hashes.
  • Notifications. If you turn on notifications, we store your device's push token so we can send them, plus your notification preferences. We record activity such as new followers and friends visiting places on your Want to Try list to show in the app.
  • Reports. If you report another user, we record the report and any details you add so we can review it. The person you report isn't told who reported them.
  • Interest in upcoming features. If you join the Book It waitlist, we record that you joined.
  • Posts you share to seva. When you share an Instagram post or web link to seva, we use the link and, where available, the post's caption to identify the business it mentions.

Information collected automatically

  • Location (only if you allow it). seva uses your device's location to show practitioners near you on the map and to find the business in a shared post. Your location is used on your device to make these searches and is not stored on our servers.
  • Usage limits. To prevent abuse, we keep a count of how often your account uses place detection.
  • Technical data. Like any internet service, our providers receive your IP address and basic request information when the app connects to them.

seva does not use advertising identifiers, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To save and sync your list across sessions.
  • To show your list to people who follow you, and to show you which friends saved a practitioner.
  • To find nearby practitioners and identify businesses from posts you share.
  • To understand interest in features we're building (such as Book It).
  • To review reports, enforce our rules and keep the service running.

Anyone with a seva account can follow you. If you'd rather your list not be visible, don't add places you want to keep private, or delete your account.

Service providers we share information with

We share information only with providers that help us run seva, and only what each needs:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, sign-in and server functions (United States).
  • Apple and Google — handle Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google.
  • Instagram — when you share a post, the app requests that post's public caption from Instagram directly from your device.
  • Anthropic — processes the caption text of posts you share to identify the business name. It receives only the caption, not your name or email.
  • Apple Maps and OpenStreetMap (Overpass API) — receive map search requests, including the area you're searching, to return nearby places.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, saved list, ratings, notes, follows, blocks and waitlist status, and revokes seva's access to your Apple ID if you used Sign in with Apple. Reports you made about others are kept without your name so we can finish reviewing them.

Business listings (a salon's name and address, for example) are shared across seva so others can save them too. If you were the first to add a business, the listing stays but is no longer linked to you.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted so each user can only read and change their own private data, and sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain.

Children

seva is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can edit your profile and delete your account in the app; for anything else, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above and let you know in the app.
